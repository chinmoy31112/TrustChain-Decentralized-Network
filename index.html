Simple & Transparent

How It Works

Step 01

Connect Wallet

Connect MetaMask, Phantom, OKX, Coinbase, or any Web3 wallet to authenticate.

Step 02

Browse or Create

Explore verified campaigns or launch your own in minutes using our smart contract.

Step 03

Donate with MNT

Send MNT directly to the campaign smart contract. No intermediaries, minimal fees.

Step 04

Get Your NFT

Receive an on-chain NFT receipt (Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Diamond) — your permanent proof of generosity.

Why Mantle Network

Built with Trust at the Core

Traditional charity platforms take 5–30% in fees and require blind trust. TrustChain uses Mantle Network smart contracts to guarantee every donation reaches its destination with full transparency.

  • Low gas fees on Mantle Network
  • Immutable on-chain donation records
  • Creator can only withdraw after goal or deadline
  • Community governance voting on campaigns
  • NFT receipts as proof of donation
  • Open-source smart contracts, auditable by anyone
