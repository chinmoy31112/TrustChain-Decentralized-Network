How It Works

Connect your MetaMask wallet to authenticate without any signup or personal data.

🌍

Why Blockchain

Built with Trust at the Core

Traditional charity platforms take 5–30% in fees and require blind trust. CharityFund uses Ethereum smart contracts to guarantee every donation reaches its destination with full transparency.

  • Zero platform fees — gas only
  • Immutable on-chain donation records
  • Creator can only withdraw after goal or deadline
  • Community governance voting on campaigns
  • NFT receipts as tax-deductible proof
  • Open-source smart contracts, auditable by anyone
