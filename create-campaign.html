⚠️ Before you submit:

  • Campaigns are deployed permanently on Ethereum blockchain
  • You can only withdraw funds after the goal is met or deadline passes
  • Donors will receive NFT receipts automatically
  • All transactions require gas fees (ETH)

📌 Campaign Tips

  • Use a high-quality, relevant image
  • Set a realistic, achievable goal
  • Write a compelling, honest description
  • Share your campaign on social media
  • Keep donors updated on progress
  • Be specific about fund usage
